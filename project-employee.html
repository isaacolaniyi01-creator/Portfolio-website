Employee Retention & Attrition Analysis

Understanding drivers of turnover and retention strategies. Early-stage write-up

Storytelling

Analysis of employee records to identify factors correlated with attrition: tenure, role, performance rating, and manager tenure.

Formulas

Attrition Rate: Attrition = (Employees who left during period) / (Average employees during period)

Hazard Rate: used for modeling time-to-event (turnover).

Findings

  1. Higher attrition among early-career hires in sales roles.
  2. Manager changes correlate with a short-term spike in turnover.

Recommended Actions

  1. Strengthen onboarding and mentorship for early-career hires.
  2. Improve manager training to reduce turnover after manager changes.
