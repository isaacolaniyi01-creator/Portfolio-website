Customer Demographics & Churn Analysis

Understanding which customer groups leave, and why. Early-stage write-up

Storytelling

We trace customer cohorts, demographics, and behaviors to understand churn drivers and to prioritize retention investments.

Formulas

Churn Rate: Churn = (Customers at start of period − Customers at end of period) / Customers at start

Survival Function (Kaplan-Meier): useful for estimating customer lifetime.

Findings

  1. Higher churn among customers with low initial order value and infrequent purchases.
  2. Demographic segments under 30 years old show higher churn but higher responsiveness to discounts.

Recommended Actions

  1. Introduce win-back offers for customers inactive for 60–90 days.
  2. Personalize onboarding for new customers to increase early engagement.
